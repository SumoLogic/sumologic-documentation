The alert details page now organizes alert information into tabs, so you can find the root cause of an alert faster. [Learn more](/docs/alerts/monitors/alert-response/).

**What's new:**
* **Alert Details** tab shows an AI verdict, key details such as severity, status, entity, and duration, the triggering query, a chart, the raw log messages, and alert context cards.
* **AI Investigation** tab shows the result of an automated or manual AI investigation, explains what caused the alert, and lists recommended actions. Continue the investigation in Mobot with the alert context.
* **Relevant Alerts** tab lists alerts that fired around the same time or on the same entity.
* **Monitor History** tab lists previous occurrences of the alert.
* **View in Metrics Explorer** opens the alert's metrics query in Metrics Explorer.
