Sumo Logic now validates the `Destination` URL in the SAML response from your identity provider (IdP) against the Assertion Consumer Service (ACS) URL configured in your Sumo Logic SAML configuration. This follows [Section 3.5.5.2 of the SAML 2.0 Bindings specification](https://docs.oasis-open.org/security/saml/v2.0/saml-bindings-2.0-os.pdf#page=24) and strengthens the security of your single sign-on (SSO) setup. [Learn more](/docs/manage/security/saml/saml-destination-url-mismatch/).

**Rollout:**
* **November 16, 2026.** Users in organizations with a mismatch see a warning once per session at login. Logins are not blocked.
* **January 29, 2027.** Sumo Logic rejects logins with a mismatched Destination URL.

:::important
SSO administrators need to set both the ACS URL and the Destination URL in their IdP to match the **Assertion Consumer** URL on the **SAML** page in Sumo Logic before January 29, 2027.
:::
