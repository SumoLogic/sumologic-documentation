We're excited to introduce dynamic timeslice drilldown for dashboard log panels. When you click and drag to zoom into a time range, Sumo Logic now automatically recalculates the query's timeslice and re-fetches the panel at that finer resolution, so you can drill into spikes and anomalies without editing the underlying query.

[Learn more](/docs/dashboards/set-custom-time-ranges/#drill-down-on-a-log-panel-by-zooming).
