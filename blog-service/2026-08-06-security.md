We're excited to announce that only active threat intelligence indicators are available for search and Cloud SIEM entity enrichment by default. Expired indicators stay in your threat intelligence data store until their retention period ends, but they're excluded from the Threat Intelligence page, log search, and entity enrichment unless you explicitly include them. This is in rollout phase and will be available in all regions shortly. [Learn more](/docs/security/threat-intelligence/about-threat-intelligence).
