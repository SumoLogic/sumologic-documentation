We're excited to announce improvements to how threat intelligence indicators are searched:
- By default, only active threat intelligence indicators are available for search and Cloud SIEM entity enrichment. [Learn more](/docs/security/threat-intelligence/about-threat-intelligence).
- You can now use the `threatlookup` operator to search threat intelligence indicators in your log data. [Learn more](/docs/search/search-query-language/search-operators/threatlookup/).

This is in the rollout phase and will be available in all regions shortly.
