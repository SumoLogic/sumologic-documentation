Effective November 30, 2026, the sender address for email notifications from Monitors, Scheduled Searches, and Automation Service will change to `noreply@notifications.sumologic.com` to strengthen security and reduce phishing risk.

If you use email filters, routing rules, allowlists, or downstream integrations that rely on the current sender address (`service@sumologic.com`), update them before November 13, 2026 to avoid notification disruption. Notification content and delivery timing will remain unchanged.
