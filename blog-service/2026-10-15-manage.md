On November 30, 2026, Sumo Logic will change the sender address for email notifications from Monitors, Scheduled Searches, and Automation Service to `noreply@notifications.sumologic.com` to strengthen security and reduce phishing risk.

If you use email filters, routing rules, allowlists, or downstream integrations that rely on the current sender address (`service@sumologic.com`), update them before November 13, 2026 to avoid notification disruption. Notification content and delivery timing will remain unchanged, and no action is required unless your configurations depend on the current sender address.
