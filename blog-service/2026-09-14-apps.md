### Global Intelligence AWS apps - Deprecating in the coming weeks

The following Global Intelligence Service apps will be deprecated and removed from the App Catalog:

- **Global Intelligence for Amazon GuardDuty**
- **Global Intelligence for AWS CloudTrail DevOps**
- **Global Intelligence for AWS CloudTrail SecOps**

Additionally, Global Intelligence for AWS CloudTrail DevOps will also be removed from the AWS Observability Solution.

### Opsgenie - Deprecating April 5, 2027

The following Opsgenie-related services will be deprecated on April 5, 2027 and removed from the App Catalog:

- **Opsgenie App**. Removed from the App Catalog and no longer available as a standalone experience after this date.
- **Atlassian Opsgenie Automation Service integration**. Deprecated on this date.
