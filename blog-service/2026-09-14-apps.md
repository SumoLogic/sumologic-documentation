The following Global Intelligence Service apps will be deprecated and removed from the App Catalog in the coming weeks:

- Global Intelligence for Amazon GuardDuty.
- Global Intelligence for AWS CloudTrail DevOps.
- Global Intelligence for AWS CloudTrail SecOps.

The Global Intelligence for AWS CloudTrail DevOps app will also be removed from the AWS Observability Solution.
