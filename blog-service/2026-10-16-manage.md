Email notifications from [Monitors](/docs/alerts/monitors/create-monitor/), [Scheduled Searches](/docs/alerts/scheduled-searches/create-email-alert), and [Automation Service](/docs/platform-services/automation-service/playbooks/create-playbooks) are now sent from `noreply@notifications.sumologic.com`. Allowlist this address and update any email filters, inbox rules, distribution lists, or automated workflows that depend on the previous sender.

Only the sender address changes. Subject lines, email templates, recipients, attachments, notification content, and delivery logic stay the same. Account sign-up, email verification and change, password reset, and other transactional account emails are not affected and keep their existing sender.
