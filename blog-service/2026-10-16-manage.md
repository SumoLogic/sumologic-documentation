Metrics transformation rules now offer updated retention options. Retention for matching metrics is **Do Not Store** or **400 days**, and aggregate retention for aggregated metrics is standardized to **400 days**. Rules that already use 15-day retention get migrated to 400 days. [Learn more](/docs/metrics/metrics-transformation-rules/).
