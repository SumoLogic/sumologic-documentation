The new **Value Overview** tab under **Administration** > **Account** shows the estimated time Mobot, the SOC Analyst Agent, and the Root Cause Analysis Agent save your team. You can review a combined time total, drill into each metric's formula and supporting data, and adjust the manual-time assumptions behind the estimates. [Learn more](/docs/manage/manage-subscription/value-overview/).

**What's new:**
* **Combined Time Reclaimed.** See total estimated hours saved across all three agents, with a breakdown by agent.
* **Agent metrics.** Review time saved, investigation activity, and time to verdict for each agent.
* **Filters and search.** Filter by source and search for metrics within a date range you choose.
* **Configurable assumptions.** Edit the manual-time values used in hours saved calculations.

:::important
You need the **View Value Overview** capability to see the page and the **Manage Value Overview** capability to change assumptions.
:::
