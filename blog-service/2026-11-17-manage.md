You can now see which teams, services, and environments drive your Sumo Logic ingest, and who owns that usage. The new **Usage Attribution** tab on the **Account Overview** page provides an out-of-the-box dashboard that breaks down ingest volume by data tier and by fields such as `_sourceCategory`, collector, and host. [Learn more](/docs/manage/ingestion-volume/usage-attribution/).

- **Attribution dashboard.** View volume by tier, top consumers, trends over time, and the share of volume that is still unassigned.
- **Custom tags.** Add your own tags, such as environment or cost center, and use them as breakdown dimensions. A new tag applies to data ingested after you save it.
- **Ownership lookup.** Upload a CSV that maps a field value to team, owner, and other columns, and then break down usage by those columns.
- **Saved filters and breakdown.** Your filters and **Breakdown by** selection are saved, so they remain when you reload the page.

Usage Attribution reports volume and data tier. It does not calculate dollar or credit costs.
