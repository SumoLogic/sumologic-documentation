### New release

#### AWSO v3.0.0

We're excited to announce the release of AWSO v3.0.0:
- **Complete rewrite** of the AWSO solution.
- **Expanded AWS Region support**, including non-SAM regions and newly supported regions: Asia Pacific (Melbourne), Asia Pacific (New Zealand), and Europe (Zurich).
- **Support for AWS Regions launched after August 2022**, with the appropriate Load Balancer policies configured automatically.
- **Terraform Module support** for solution deployment, including release versioning.
- **Next-generation application support** with out-of-the-box dashboards, monitors, FERs, and metric rules.
[Learn more](/docs/observability/aws/deploy-use-aws-observability/v3.0.0/)
