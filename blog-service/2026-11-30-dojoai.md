Dojo Studio now includes settings for configuring each agent, including general settings, instructions, knowledge, memories, skills, permissions, sharing, integrations, and tasks. Org administrators and users with Manage access can tune how the SOC Analyst Agent, Root Cause Agent, and custom agents work for your team. [Learn more](/docs/get-started/dojo-studio).

Agent Knowledge is now generally available for the Root Cause Agent and SOC Analyst Agent, and in public preview for custom agents. Give an agent persistent knowledge about your environment, operational workflows, and team judgment. Teach the agent once, and it applies that knowledge to every auto-investigation and Mobot follow-up, with full transparency into which knowledge shaped each verdict.
