The 15-day retention and 15-day aggregate retention options have been removed from Metrics Transformation Rules. Retention for matching metrics is now **Do Not Store** or **400 days**, and aggregate retention for aggregated metrics is now **400 days**.

**What's new:**
* The **Retention** field for a metrics transformation rule now offers **Do Not Store** or **400 days**
* The **Aggregate Retention** field for aggregated metrics is now always **400 days**
* Existing rules configured with 15-day retention or 15-day aggregate retention are no longer supported

[Learn more](/docs/metrics/metrics-transformation-rules/).
