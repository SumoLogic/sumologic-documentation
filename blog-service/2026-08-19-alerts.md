We're excited to announce multiple notification support for Scheduled Searches. Previously limited to one alert type, you can now add up to 10 notifications to a single Scheduled Search. These alerts share the same trigger condition and fire from the same search execution, with each alert configured independently. [Learn more](/docs/alerts/scheduled-searches/schedule-search/#step-6-add-scheduled-search-alert-types).
