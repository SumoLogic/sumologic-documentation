**Root Cause Agent**, one of Sumo Logic's [DojoAI](/docs/get-started/ai-machine-learning/#dojoai) agents, is now generally available. [Learn more](/docs/alerts/monitors/root-cause-agent).

Root Cause Agent investigates observability alerts: start an investigation from the **Alerts** list or an alert's details page, and the agent gathers telemetry on its own, follows the signal across your logs and metrics, and returns a verdict with supporting evidence.

**What's new:**
* **AI Verdict, What Happened, Key Findings, and Recommended Actions** on a new **AI Investigation** tab, with each finding linked to the log search or metrics query behind it
* **Investigation trace**, a step-by-step record of what the agent checked and found
* **Related Alerts** and **Monitor History** tabs for broader context on an alert
* Continue investigating conversationally in **Mobot**, with the investigation's full context already loaded

Root Cause Agent investigations draw from your organization's [DojoAI Credits](/docs/get-started/ai-machine-learning/#dojoai-credits) pool. Contact your account team for pricing and promotional details.
