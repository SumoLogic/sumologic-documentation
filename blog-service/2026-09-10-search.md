We're excited to introduce two ways to run a search in the background, so you don't have to keep the **Search** page open while you wait. If a log search's elapsed time exceeds about a minute, a banner prompts you to click **Run in Background**, and you'll receive an email notification with a link to the results as soon as it completes. For searches that finish in under a minute, click the three-dot kebab icon and select **Run in Background (Pin)** to pin it yourself, no email needed since the search is already done. [Learn more](/docs/search/get-started-with-search/search-page/pin-a-search/).
