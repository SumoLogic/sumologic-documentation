We're excited to announce that when a log search's elapsed time exceeds about a minute, Sumo Logic now prompts you to run it in the background instead of waiting. Once pinned, you'll receive an email notification with a link to the results as soon as the search completes, so you can close the **Search** page and check back later. [Learn more](/docs/search/get-started-with-search/search-page/pin-a-search/#run-a-slow-search-in-the-background-automatically).
