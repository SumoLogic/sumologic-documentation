We're excited to introduce one-click drill-down for [map charts](/docs/dashboards/panels/map-charts/). *Shift+click* a point or line on the map to drill down into that location's data. Sumo Logic redirects you to the Log Search page with the query already updated with the corresponding latitude and longitude values.

Previously, you needed to manually find the IP address, or the latitude and longitude, for a location and then edit the query yourself to drill down into that location's data. *Shift+click* now does this for you automatically. [Learn more](/docs/dashboards/panels/map-charts/).
