The Sumo Logic app for AI Observability is now available in Limited Availability, providing early access to observability capabilities designed to monitor and understand AI workloads. This release offers an opportunity to explore the capabilities and share feedback as we continue to enhance the experience ahead of its planned transition to an in-product experience.
The app provides preconfigured dashboards and monitors to track the health, performance, cost, and behavior of AI and LLM applications using OpenTelemetry trace spans enriched with GenAI Semantic Convention (`gen_ai.*`) attributes. [Learn more](/docs/integrations/aiml/ai-observability/ai-observability-app/).

For additional information, see the instrumentation guides for [OpenLLMetry](/docs/integrations/aiml/ai-observability/instrument-your-llm-application-with-openllmetry/) and [OpenTelemetry](/docs/integrations/aiml/ai-observability/instrument-your-llm-application-with-opentelemetry/).
