In-app tabs now support Dashboards and Metrics in the New UI, in addition to Log Search. You can keep Log Search, Dashboard, and Metrics tabs open together in a single browser window and switch between them without losing context. This makes it easier to move between investigating logs, reviewing dashboards, and exploring metrics without opening extra browser tabs. In-app tabs are available if you have enabled the **Enable in-app tabs** preference. [Learn more](/docs/get-started/account-settings-preferences/#in-app-tabs).
