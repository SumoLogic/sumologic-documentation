Mobot can now answer natural-language questions about your Sumo Logic ingest volume. The Data Volume skill is part of Mobot's Platform Optimization Agent and is available to all users with no separate opt-in required. Admins can use it to identify noisy sources and explain month-over-month fluctuations, and finance teams can use it to attribute spend, reconcile credits, and forecast renewals.

Ask questions like `What are my top 10 source categories by daily ingest volume?` or `How much data went to the Infrequent tier last month?` and Mobot queries the Data Volume Index and returns a structured answer in the unit and breakdown you asked for — no manual query writing or support ticket required. [Learn more](/docs/manage/ingestion-volume/data-volume-index/).
