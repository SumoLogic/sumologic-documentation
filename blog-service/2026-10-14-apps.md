The Sumo Logic app for Google Workspace Activities collects audit events from the Google Admin SDK Reports API to help monitor user and administrator activity across your Google Workspace environment.
Explore predefined dashboards for productivity services, including Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Meet, and Gemini, as well as security, identity, and administration activities. [Learn more](/docs/integrations/google/workspace-activities).
