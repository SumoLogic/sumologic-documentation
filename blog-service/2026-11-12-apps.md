The Sumo Logic app for AI Observability is now in limited availability before we transition it to an in-product experience in the future. It provides preconfigured dashboards and monitors to track the health, performance, cost, and behavior of your AI and LLM applications, based on OpenTelemetry trace spans that carry GenAI Semantic Convention (`gen_ai.*`) attributes. [Learn more](/docs/integrations/aiml/ai-observability/ai-observability-app/).

For more information, see the instrumentation guides for [OpenLLMetry](/docs/integrations/aiml/ai-observability/instrument-your-llm-application-with-openllmetry/) and [OpenTelemetry](/docs/integrations/aiml/ai-observability/instrument-your-llm-application-with-opentelemetry/).
