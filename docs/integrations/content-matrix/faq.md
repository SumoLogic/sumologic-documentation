Use these answers to understand what the [Content Matrix](/docs/integrations/content-matrix/) lists and how to find the integrations you need.

## How many vendors and products does Sumo Logic integrate with?

Sumo Logic integrates with hundreds of vendors and products. The Content Matrix splits them alphabetically across two articles: [Content Matrix A-L](/docs/integrations/content-matrix/content-matrix-a-l/) and [Content Matrix M-Z](/docs/integrations/content-matrix/content-matrix-m-z/).

## What types of integrations does Sumo Logic offer?

Depending on the vendor, Sumo Logic offers apps (pre-built dashboards), automation integrations (App Catalog playbooks and actions), Cloud SIEM integrations (parsers and rules), and collectors (sources for ingesting data). For descriptions of each type, see [Content Matrix](/docs/integrations/content-matrix/).

## Is my vendor or product supported by Sumo Logic?

Search [Content Matrix A-L](/docs/integrations/content-matrix/content-matrix-a-l/) and [Content Matrix M-Z](/docs/integrations/content-matrix/content-matrix-m-z/) for the vendor's name. If you do not see it listed, check with your Sumo Logic account team, since new integrations are added regularly.

## Can a single vendor have multiple types of Sumo Logic integrations?

Yes. Many vendors, such as AWS and Atlassian, have several integration types at once, including apps, automation integrations, Cloud SIEM integrations, and collectors. Each one is linked separately in the matrix.

## Are community-built integrations included in the Content Matrix?

Yes. Some entries link to community apps built and maintained outside of Sumo Logic's official app catalog, in addition to the officially supported apps, automation integrations, and collectors.
