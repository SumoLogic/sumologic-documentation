In this release, we've enhanced the security and stability of the Collector with upgraded JRE and security patches.

#### Security fixes

- Upgraded Collector JRE to **Amazon Corretto Version 17.0.19.10.1**.
- Upgraded `io.netty` packages to **4.1.136.Final** to address CVEs.
- Upgraded `com.fasterxml.jackson` library to **2.21.5** to address CVEs.
- Upgraded `log4j` packages to **2.25.5** to fix [CVE-2026-49844](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-49844).
