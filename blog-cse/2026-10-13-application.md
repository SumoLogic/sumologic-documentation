### Grok deprecation notice

Effective April 23, 2026, we've deprecated our Grok parsing service. If your instance uses Grok for parsing, you must migrate your data sources to use C2C integrations or Sumo Logic parsers before that date.

[Learn more](/docs/cse/schema/grok-deprecation-notice).
