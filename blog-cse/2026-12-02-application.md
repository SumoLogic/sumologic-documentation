## Overview tab on the Insight details page (Limited Availability)

The Insight details page now has an **Overview** tab, so you can assess an insight without switching between tabs. The tab shows panels for AI Analysis, Signals, MITRE ATT&CK, Log Activity, and Automation.

Click the gear icon on the Insight details page to show or hide the **Overview** tab and to choose which panels appear. [Learn more](/docs/cse/get-started-with-cloud-siem/about-cse-insight-ui/#overview).
