## Updated Enrichments tab in Cloud SIEM (Limited Availability)

The **Enrichments** tab on the Cloud SIEM insight details, signal details, and entity details pages has a new design. The new look makes enrichment data and threat indicator labels easier to read.

The enrichment JSON is now displayed in full, without expandable nodes.

This update is in limited availability. To request access, contact your Sumo Logic account representative.

For more information, see [Enrichments in Cloud SIEM](/docs/cse/integrations/enrichments-and-indicators).
