### Sort Cloud SIEM keyword search results

You can now sort keyword search results by relevance or by time. Results are sorted by **Relevance (desc.)** by default, which lists the best text matches first. Time-based sorting uses **Event Time** for insights and signals, and **Last Seen** for entities. Your sort is saved in the page URL, so it's preserved when you reload the page or share the link. [Learn more](/docs/cse/administration/filter-search/#sort-search-results).
